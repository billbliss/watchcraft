Explore Marc’s landscape photography tutorials by topic and chapter. Find a technique, jump to the right moment, and follow along with your own videos.

This collection organizes 176 tutorials. You’ll need your own copies of the videos and the Watchcraft desktop app; video files are not included.

Already installed? Your browser may ask permission to open Watchcraft. For the guided setup, use version 0.3.1-beta.4 or later.

Start watching in three steps

  1. Install Watchcraft, then open this collection.

    Return to this page and choose “Open in Watchcraft.” Confirm that you want to add the collection.

  2. Choose your Marc Adamus Videos folder.

    Select the main folder containing Part 1, Part 2, and the other Part folders, on your computer or an external drive. No Watchcraft files need to be stored beside your videos.

  3. Review the videos found and start watching.

    You can start with a partial library. For offline use, make sure the video files are downloaded to your device or external drive before you travel.

Watchcraft didn’t open?

Open Settings in the desktop app and paste this collection URL into “Add a collection”:
