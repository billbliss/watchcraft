Watchcraft

Learn a craft by watching.

Find the exact lesson where a technique is taught, move directly to its chapter, and turn a folder—or a public video course—into a searchable learning library.

Getting started

A collection can carry a small managed video, refer to videos already on your computer, or play remote sources such as YouTube.

Download the version of Watchcraft for your computer and install it.
Choose a collection below and open it directly in Watchcraft, or copy its URL and paste it into Watchcraft Settings.
Browse the collection in Watchcraft, locate any local videos it needs, and begin learning.
