Getting started

A collection can carry a small managed video, refer to videos already on your computer, or play web video such as YouTube.

Open a web-video collection instantly in your browser—there is nothing to install.
For collections that use videos on your computer, download the desktop app for macOS, Windows, or Linux.
Choose a featured collection below, or add any compatible collection URL from Watchcraft Settings.
